- **Minor — `incremental.py:9–14`: Exclusion results leak across calls.** The mutable default `excluded=[]` is shared and returned directly. After `filter_excluded([".git/config"])`, calling `filter_excluded([])` still returns `[".git/config"]`. Later calls also mutate previously returned results, violating the contract that the result is a subset of the supplied paths.

- **Major — `incremental.py:20–21`: Failed copies are reported as completed.** `copy_file()` suppresses `OSError`, so `copy_many()` appends the destination even when the source is missing or the destination cannot be written. Its documented “paths done” result therefore includes unsuccessful copies; callers relying on it to record synchronization success can incorrectly mark missing or incomplete data as synced.

- **Major — `incremental.py:37–38`: Manifest updates with older timestamps are silently discarded.** Restoring a file from backup or replacing it with a timestamp-preserving copy can legitimately decrease its mtime. The merge retains the old entry even when the new size and contents differ. If that replacement was copied, the resulting manifest misrepresents the destination and subsequent change detection operates on stale state.
