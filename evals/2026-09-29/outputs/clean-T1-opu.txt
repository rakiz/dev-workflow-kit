## Points to settle before coding

1. **`skipped` is defined nowhere.** The simplest assumption: the number of source files that did not change (`len(src_manifest) - len(copied)`). This choice must be confirmed.
2. **`sync()` only returns the `to_copy` list.** It does not give the total number of scanned files. But GOAL.md forbids changing its signature. A new helper is needed, and `sync()` builds on it without changing its behavior.
3. **`copy_file` silently ignores `OSError`** (`synccli.py:68`). As a result, `copied` may list files that were not copied. The JSON passes that lie on to machines. It is a known risk, but it is out of GOAL.md's scope.
4. **One test already fails:** 1 test out of 6 (`test_first_item_stale_is_included`). The cause is `collect_stale`, which starts at `range(1, …)`. It is unrelated to the requirement, but a "all tests pass" criterion would be impossible to meet.
5. **Paths use `os.sep`.** On Windows, `copied` would contain `\`. A decision is needed: keep the native format or normalize to POSIX.

(DESIGN.md and `incremental.py` concern other workstreams: the v2 daemon and a commit review. They are out of scope, with a single link: the planned daemon would reuse `sync()`, which confirms its signature must stay stable.)
